🕯️ Prayer & Reflection

A few simple prompts for slowing down and turning your attention toward God.

🙏

Morning offering

Begin the day by offering your thoughts, choices, work, and intentions to God. Ask for the grace to choose what is good.

📖

Scripture pause

Read a short passage from Scripture. Ask: “What stands out to me, and how could I live it today?”

🌙

Night reflection

Think about one thing you did well, one thing you could improve, and one thing you are grateful for. Begin again tomorrow.
